# UI/UX Brand Direction

## Brand story
Born from the depth of the earth, Khu Mo is crafted like ore refined under pressure: raw origin, disciplined process, and rare character. This is a premium liquor brand for young professionals who want more than a drink; they want a mark of identity that feels bold, grounded, and unapologetically distinct. Every bottle embodies underground energy and industrial precision, turning hidden origins into modern prestige.

## Key messaging
- **Core brand line:** Forged Below. Owned Above.
- **Value promise:** Premium craft liquor with raw heritage and modern sophistication.
- **Audience resonance:** For ambitious urban professionals celebrating progress, grit, and late-night wins.
- **Tone pillars:** Strong, masculine, mysterious, confident.
- **Message structure:**
  - **Origin:** From underground roots, shaped by pressure.
  - **Craft:** Precision distillation, uncompromising quality.
  - **Status:** A signature pour for people building their future.
  - **Ritual:** From after-hours networking to private milestones.

## Visual direction

### Colors
- **Primary:** Charcoal Black `#111111`
- **Secondary:** Iron Gray `#2B2B2B`
- **Accent:** Oxidized Copper `#8A5A3B`
- **Premium highlight:** Aged Gold `#B08D57`
- **Contrast light:** Mineral White `#EDEAE4`

### Typography
- **Headline:** Condensed bold sans-serif with industrial presence.
- **Body/UI:** Clean geometric sans-serif for modern readability.
- **Optional accent:** Sharp high-contrast serif for product names and campaign lockups.

### Imagery
- Low-key lighting, strong shadows, and high contrast.
- Material textures: stone, steel, concrete, worn metal.
- Product photography: macro details of glass, label embossing, and liquid reflections.
- Lifestyle scenes: young professionals in underground-industrial nightlife settings.

### UI behavior and composition
- Dark immersive layouts with restrained copper and gold accents.
- Large, commanding hero typography and concise copy.
- Strong CTA language with clear conversion intent.
- Subtle motion only (slow parallax, soft gleam, ambient drift), never flashy.
- Product cards should feel machined: sharp edges, layered depth, premium material cues.

## UI/UX implementation notes
- Prefer a hero-centric and conversion-optimized page structure for landing pages.
- Keep visual hierarchy tight: one dominant message, one primary CTA above the fold.
- Maintain accessibility contrast for text and controls across dark surfaces.
- Reuse this file as the visual source of truth for all redesign tickets and component specs.
